# Audit, 18 October 2026: from reliability to the screens

A full review of the app once the six features Colly picked on 16 October
were built (quotes and money, kit from the notes, freelancers' own
calendars, the Crewbox show pack, departments and reading by role, vans
and drivers; [ADRs 0042 to 0047](adr/)). The question is the whole of it:
does it stay right and stay up at the company's real size, is it safe, does
it reach a phone on poor signal, and does each screen do its one job for
the person using it.

**How it was done.** Four reviews side by side, all against commit
e65ad33 and all local: nothing live was read or changed, nothing was
written to Google, and nobody was contacted.

- **Reliability and data.** The sync engine, the background jobs and the
  server read line by line; a server seeded through its own push route at
  real size (800 jobs, 1,182 phases over 1,887 phase-days, 362 calls, 700
  offers, 100 people, 3,000 items, 1,500 kit lines, 200 quotes: 9,240
  commands) on PGlite and on a private Postgres 16; a proxy adding 5 to 20
  ms each way to stand in for the distance from Railway to Neon; races
  between devices driven with the app's own sync client.
- **Security and privacy.** Sign-in, roles, private links, every place a
  rate, token or address could leave the server, the calendar fetcher's
  guard, every server-built page and CSV, headers, dependencies, erasure.
- **Screens.** Every main screen on a phone (390 px) and a desk (1440 px),
  dark and light, as each role, with the made-up data, with 300 more jobs
  and a 40-person call with 300 kit lines, and empty; contrast, focus,
  targets, the accessibility tree, offline, print.
- **Speed and running it.** Bundle sizes, a new phone on slow and fast 3G
  with the CPU slowed four times, the service worker's update flow, a long
  session, storage running out, coverage, the deploy settings and the docs
  against the code.

Priority: **P0** data loss, a security hole, or the office can't work from
it; **P1** before the next real use or bring-in; **P2** worth doing; **P3**
polish. Screen findings also carry the Nielsen Norman severity (NN 0 to
4). Personas: **D** director, **O** office, **H** head of department,
**W** warehouse, **S** staff crew, **F** freelancer. Line numbers are as of
e65ad33.

## The short version

Most of it holds. Each change is applied exactly once and a resend gets
the stored answer; a change from an older or newer app is turned down on
its own; shutdown is clean with a push in flight; day maths survive both
clock changes (25 October 2026 and 28 March 2027); a change queued offline
survives an offline reload and a deploy; memory stays flat over a long
session; the server suite passed twice and the browser suite 107 of 107.
The security review found no hole: every route needs a session, rates,
links, money and calendar addresses are cut by role in the feed, the
history and the export, every page and CSV is escaped, and the calendar
fetcher's guard holds. On the screens, contrast passes everywhere in both
themes, nothing scrolls sideways at 390 px even with 60-character names,
and refusals are plain, in place and keep what was typed.

What the audit found is in four places:

- **Two ways a device can fall out of step for good** (P0). A pull that
  finds nothing new can move a device's cursor past a change that landed
  during the pull, so the device says "Up to date" and never gets it. And
  a phone that saves up a few hundred changes can never finish sending them
  at Railway-to-Neon distance, so it shows "No signal" for ever and stops
  hearing from the server.
- **Backups and error alerts are still off** (P0), now with about 900 real
  jobs and 91 real people, and none of the problems above would raise an
  alert even with Sentry on.
- **The server and the phone at real size** (P1). Nothing is compressed,
  so a new phone on slow 3G waits 25 s for Home and 74 s for its copy (8 s
  and 18 s compressed); one write lock, a pool of ten and no database time
  limits mean one long change stalls every device; one freelancer's
  calendar can freeze the server for 26 s; an edit made offline quietly
  overwrites a newer one; Jobs and Crew take 1.3 s and 2 s to open on a
  phone.
- **Screens that don't yet fit the role or the volume** (P1). A new screen
  opens wherever the last was scrolled to; staff crew and the warehouse see
  Change and Offer buttons their role can't use, and are told only after
  typing; Crew › Calls is 22,639 px on a phone because filled calls stay
  in it.

## Findings

### P0: before anything else

**1. A device can miss a change for good when it pulls just as another
change lands.** D O H W S. The pull reads its page in one query and the
head of the feed in another (`server/src/app.ts:304-330`); when the page
is empty, the cursor jumps to the head. A change committed between the two
queries is never sent. With the app's own sync client on Postgres, two
phones scanning 150 labels each while a laptop synced: the laptop said
"Up to date" at cursor 300 and held 296 (299 on a second run). The missing
record stays missing until it changes again, and it's likeliest when the
place is busiest. **Fix:** an empty page keeps the device's own cursor;
a regression test that commits between the two reads; one full re-read
for every device, in case one already skipped something.

*Fixed, 18 October 2026* ([ADR 0001](adr/0001-sync-engine.md), amended).
An empty page keeps the device's cursor; a test slips a change in between
the two reads and the next pull brings it; each pull says `FEED` 2, and a
copy read before reads the feed again from the start, once, keeping its
outbox.

**2. A phone with a long backlog is stuck for ever, and hears nothing
while stuck.** W S H, and every device after a restore. A phone sends 500
changes a push and waits 30 s (`shared/src/protocol.ts:22`,
`web/src/transport.ts:15`); at 15 queries each and 20 ms a round trip the
server needs well over a minute. The phone gives up, sends the same 500
again, and the resend, though every one is already applied, also outlasts
30 s, every time. A pull only runs after a push succeeds
(`shared/src/sync/client.ts:455-456`), so the phone never receives
anyone else's changes either, and nothing is logged. **Fix:** push 50 at
a time, check the whole push for repeats in one query, let a failed push
still pull, and report repeated push failures from the device.

*Fixed, 18 October 2026*, but for the reporting ([ADR
0001](adr/0001-sync-engine.md), amended). The device sends 50 at a time
and the server still takes 500 from older phones; repeats are found in one
query and answered as before; a push is answered after 20 seconds with
what's done, in order, and the rest goes first next round; a failed push
still pulls. A test with every database trip slowed drains 500 changes in
pushes that each answer inside the phone's wait. Reporting repeated push
failures waits on error alerts (finding 19).

**3. Backups and error alerts are still off.** Everyone. Open since the
September audit, and now with real data: anything noticed after Neon's own
history window is gone; the erasures list lives only in the database, so
winding Neon back could bring an erased person back; documents' files
aren't kept; and crashes, 5xx errors, job faults and every device-side
error go unseen (`server/src/index.ts:89-100`, `monitoring.ts:111`).
**Fix:** Colly adds the five `BACKUP_S3_` variables, `BACKUP_KEY` and
`SENTRY_DSN` ([backups.md](backups.md), [monitoring.md](monitoring.md));
until then, Download everything once a week. Findings 1, 2, 4 and 13
would raise no alert even with Sentry on (finding 19).

### P1: before the next real use or bring-in

**4. One freelancer's calendar can freeze the server for everyone.** All.
Repeating events are expanded from their first occurrence, up to 5,000
each, on the server's only thread (`server/src/crew/busy.ts:27,88-93`):
300 daily repeats in a 34 KB file block it for 26 s, on sharing, on every
three-hourly read and on "Read it now". Any link holder can trigger it.
**Fix:** start each expansion at the window, cap the work per file, and
parse in a worker thread. (The same fix closes finding 25.)

**5. An edit made offline quietly overwrites a newer one.** O H W S.
Changes apply in arrival order, with no check of what the device last saw
(`server/src/projects/handlers.ts:37-43,123-129`). Monday's job sheet
typed on a phone with no signal replaced Tuesday's office version when the
phone came back, and neither device showed a problem. The same holds for
status, phases, people and venues. **Fix:** send the version the device
last saw with free-text and status changes, and turn a stale one down as a
conflict in words, keeping the typed text to copy.

**6. Nothing is compressed.** W S H O, NN 3. `@fastify/static` is
registered without pre-compressed files and there is no compression for
JSON (`server/src/app.ts:407-411`); the first copy is 2 to 3.4 MB where it
could be a tenth of that. On slow 3G a new phone waits 25 s for Home and
74 s for its copy, and a 195 KB page at 128 kbit/s outlasts the pull's
10 s wait, so on the worst signal it may never finish. **Fix:** build
`.br` and `.gz` files and serve them pre-compressed; `@fastify/compress`
for `/api/*`, the pull above all.

**7. One long change stalls every device.** Everyone. Every write takes
one advisory lock (`app.ts:279`, `kernel.ts:141`), a bring-in holds it for
its whole run (`calendar/import.ts:451,515`), and the pool is ten
connections with no connection, statement, lock or idle-transaction time
limits (`server/src/db.ts:43`). Held for 15 s with 12 pushes waiting,
pulls and `/api/health` answered at 15.6 s, past the phones' 10 s wait, so
every phone showed "No signal"; a transaction that stuck would hold
everything until a restart. **Fix:** time limits on the pool, a lock time
limit on pushes so a waiting one answers "busy, try again", a small pool
of its own for reads, and imports taking the lock a batch at a time.

**8. A new screen opens where the last one was scrolled to.** D O H W S,
NN 3. The heading is focused without scrolling (`web/src/Shell.tsx:131`)
and only the desk's two-column view resets. Scroll Jobs, tap Home: Home
opens at 1,203 px. Add job from the foot of Jobs: the new job opens at its
Quote card. The title and the strip that say where you are are off-screen.
**Fix:** scroll to the top when moving to a new screen, and keep each
screen's place for Back.

**9. Staff crew and the warehouse are offered buttons their role can't
use.** H W S, NN 3. Staff crew can change only their leave and report
faults (`shared/src/roles.ts:50`), yet see seven Change links and Add kit
on a job, and Offer…, Noted, Confirm and Release on Crew; the warehouse
sees nine Changes. Change opens the whole form, and Save says "Only the
office and directors…" after the typing. `roles.ts:5` promises "a button
that can't work isn't offered". **Fix:** gate each control with the
device's role check, as the job's own Change details already is.

**10. Crew › Calls lists filled calls too.** O H, NN 3. 3,093 px on a
phone with the made-up data, 22,639 px with 300 more jobs. Open since
16 October (finding 13 there). **Fix:** only calls short of people, with
"Show N filled calls" folded under them.

**11. Jobs and Crew are slow on a phone at real size.** O H W S, NN 3.
1.3 s and 2.0 s to open with the CPU slowed four times; every change
rebuilds every view (`client.ts:403-447`, kit alone 89 ms on a desk) and
saves the whole snapshot. People will tap twice. **Fix:** build views per
screen as they're needed, rebuild the kit day map only for what changed,
draw long lists in windows, save the snapshot a table at a time.

**12. A person's role follows their email, and roles below director can
change that email.** D O. Each request finds its role by matching the
account's email to a person (`server/src/auth/roles.ts:15`,
`shared/src/roles.ts:216-231`). `person.upsert` needs only the crew right
that heads hold and writes email and kind unchecked
(`server/src/crew/handlers.ts:266-297`); `person.contact` sets an email and
can be sent from a freelancer's own link (`crew/links.ts:380-386`). So
which account is the director can be changed without the director, and a
freelancer could give themselves a member of staff's address. Inferred
from the code, not reproduced. **Fix:** refuse a change to the email or
kind of a person who holds a role unless the sender can change company
settings, refuse it from a link, and refuse an email another person
already uses.

### P2: worth doing

**13. Every backup's test restore stalls the server for over 3 s.** It
boots a database before checking the file (`backup/service.ts:239-249`).
Harmless at 02:00; "Back up now" and "Start fresh" run in the day.
**Fix:** check the file first; restore in a worker. The same boots are
why `backup.test.ts` and `data.test.ts` time out under load (four to eight
databases a test); one migrated database per worker, copied, would fix
the flakes.

*Fixed, 20 October 2026* ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-the-audits-p2-and-p3s.md)). The file is checked before any database boots, and the test restore runs in a worker: the server is held up about 50 ms. Tests share one database a file.

**14. Every deploy makes every phone download about 1 MB again.** W S F.
The version is compiled into the Shell chunk (`jobs/ShowPack.tsx:135`,
`errorsSdk.ts`), so every file's name changes; 8 deploys on 16 October
were about 8 MB a phone, and the update bar eight times. **Fix:** read the
version at run time from `/api/config`, split Shell by area, and leave the
error reporter out of the precache while it's off.

*Fixed, 20 October 2026* ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-the-audits-p2-and-p3s.md)). The version is read from `version.json` beside the page; a deploy that changes no screen now changes 2 KB on a phone, not 1.2 MB. React and Leaflet have chunks of their own and the error reporter isn't precached while it's off. Splitting Shell by area was tried and dropped: the areas import each other.

**15. Every device polls every 30 s even with the live connection up.** O
D. `web/src/sync.ts:49`, three queries a poll, two log lines a poll; the
own-calendar job also queries every 15 minutes with nobody sharing.
Inferred: this keeps Neon awake. **Fix:** poll only while the connection
is down, skip the job's query when there's nothing to read, log only
failed and slow requests.

*Fixed, 20 October 2026* ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-the-audits-p2-and-p3s.md)). Devices poll only while the live connection is down, with a heartbeat to notice a dead one; the calendar job asks nothing until one is due; the server logs only failed and slow requests.

**16. Phones don't ask to keep their storage.** W S, NN 2. No
`navigator.storage.persist()` anywhere; Safari clears an origin unused for
seven days unless it's on the home screen, and Chrome evicts when space
runs low, taking the outbox with it. **Fix:** ask after sign-in, and say
on You whether it's kept.

*Fixed, 20 October 2026* ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-the-audits-p2-and-p3s.md)). Phones ask to keep their storage once someone is in, and You says whether this browser keeps the copy.

**17. A new phone replays the whole feed, and the feed only grows.** W S
H. No compaction; 6,778 changes for 5,720 live records here, more on live
with the first import taken back. **Fix:** answer a first pull with the
current records and the head, keeping the feed for what's new.

*Fixed, 20 October 2026* ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-the-audits-p2-and-p3s.md)). A first pull asks for the latest of each record, page by page, with nothing committed during the read skipped; tests show every role's copy the same as a full replay, from under half the changes.

**18. The docs promise what the code doesn't do.** D O.
[Architecture](architecture.md) still says each device syncs only its
scope (every staff device holds the whole company, money, rates and links
aside, including every person's phone and email), that phones use SQLite,
that jobs run on a queue, that free text uses Yjs, and that backups and
alerts run from day one. **Fix:** mark those as planned or not built, and
say plainly which roles' phones hold everyone's contact details. (ADRs
0042 to 0047 match the code.)

*Fixed, 20 October 2026* ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-the-audits-p2-and-p3s.md)). [Architecture](architecture.md) and the README now say what's built: which role's device holds whose phone and email, IndexedDB, timers, no Yjs, and when backups and alerts went on.

**19. What the error reports would never see.** Findings 1, 2, 4 and 13
raise no error anywhere. **Fix:** report from the device on repeated push
failures and an outbox older than a day; on the server, event-loop delay
over 1 s and empty pages served behind the head.

*Fixed, 20 October 2026* ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-the-audits-p2-and-p3s.md)). The server reports being held up over a second and an empty pull behind the head; devices report pushes failing while online and a change waiting over a day.

**20. A 40-person call with 25 booked says "0/2 days filled".** O H, NN 3.
And its names run down a one-word column, about 2,000 px on a phone
(`web/src/crew/CrewScreen.tsx:710`). Open since 16 October (finding 19
there). **Fix:** "25 of 40, both days", names at full width, the first
eight then "and 32 more".

*Fixed, 20 October 2026* ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-the-audits-p2-and-p3s.md)). "25 of 40, both days", the names across the card, the first eight then "and 17 more".

**21. Each short kit line has its own warning and button.** O W, NN 3. A
job with 300 lines short is 70,196 px on a phone, and while the stock list
arrives in parts "none counted yet" will be the first state of most real
jobs. **Fix:** one line per reason, compact lines, and one "Subhire what's
short…" with a list to tick.

*Fixed, 20 October 2026* ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-the-audits-p2-and-p3s.md)). One line by reason first, compact lines, and one "Subhire what's short…" with a list to tick.

**22. The Jobs list and Home are long lists on a phone.** O D, NN 2.
Jobs is 31,416 px at volume with New job at the very bottom; Home is
4,128 px with Today's four-line rows before Needs attention, and an
enquiry counts in Today. Home was finding 14 on 16 October. **Fix:**
sticky week headers, the next fortnight with Show all, New job at the top;
Home's tiles first and one line per job.

*Fixed, 20 October 2026* ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-the-audits-p2-and-p3s.md)). Jobs: sticky week headings, the next fortnight then Show all, New job at the top on a phone. Home: Needs attention before Today, one line a job, enquiries out of Today.

**23. Small things that mislead on the screens.** NN 2 each.
- On a phone the area rows hide the new pages (Stock shows four of seven,
  Vehicles off the edge; Jobs hides Money), with no cue, and the "On this
  screen" strip looks the same as the row. An edge fade, or wrap below
  600 px, and a different look for the strip.
- Crew › People has two cards called "Running out"; the vans' one should
  say "Vehicle checks running out" (`web/src/stock/Vehicles.tsx:140`).
- A head's Home still says calls don't have a department, under a tile
  counting their department's calls (`web/src/home/HomeScreen.tsx:250`).
- The freelancer's page has "Your bookings in your own calendar" right
  above "Your own calendar", and no h1 (`server/src/crew/page.ts:435`):
  "Put your bookings in your calendar", "Let us see when you're busy",
  and "Hi Dara" as the heading.
- On the demo, a narrower persona keeps what the browser already held
  until a reload: staff crew saw €683.50, a €320 rate, Approve and Agree.
  Clear the copy and read again when a persona is chosen.
- Money's tables are cut off on a phone with no cue that they scroll.

*Fixed, 20 October 2026* ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-the-audits-p2-and-p3s.md)). All six: the area rows wrap and the strip looks like contents; "Vehicle checks running out"; the head's hint counts the calls with no department; the freelancer's page has "Hi Dara" and the two clearer headings; choosing a persona reads the copy again before opening; Money's tables show a shade where there's more.

**24. Small security hardening.** The demo passcode's one-second pause
doesn't slow guesses sent together, and nothing on the server limits
requests (`auth/routes.ts:219`): a counter and a 429 after about ten wrong
tries, and a passcode of twelve or more characters. No content security
policy beyond `frame-ancestors` (`app.ts`): a strict one on the
freelancer's pages, call sheets and the app shell, as a second line behind
the escaping that already holds.

*Fixed, 20 October 2026* ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-the-audits-p2-and-p3s.md)). Ten wrong passcodes from one address in fifteen minutes gets a 429; under 8 characters refused, 8 to 11 warned. A content security policy on the app shell and a strict one on the server's own pages.

**25. Freelancers' calendars: old repeats missed, and Node not pinned.** A
daily event since 2010 gives no busy times, because the 5,000 cap is
reached before today, and time zone definitions are registered for the
whole process, so one person's file changes how the next is read
(`busy.ts:51,91`); both close with finding 4's fix. Separately, Node is
`>=22` with no `.nvmrc`, and the services' dashboard start command may
still run npm, which swallows the shutdown signal: pin `22.x` and clear
the dashboard command on both services.

*Fixed, 20 October 2026* ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-the-audits-p2-and-p3s.md)). Both already closed by ADR 0049; `.nvmrc` added. Colly still to clear the dashboard start command on both services.

**26. Tests missing on the new money code.** The freelancers' costs file
for the accountant (`shared/src/sync/money-view.ts:428-453`) has no test;
the device-side views for money, kit from the notes and vans are at 67%,
45% and 85% of lines. **Fix:** unit tests for each, and a test that runs
each command through the server and the device's pending copy and
compares them, so the two can't drift.

*Fixed, 20 October 2026* ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-the-audits-p2-and-p3s.md)). Tests on the costs file and the device views (money 88%, kit from the notes 99%, vans 100%), and a parity test over 90 commands. It fixed four small drifts and marks one left: a call's copy of its job, phase and venue names.

## How each kind of person gets on

- **Director.** Money answers "are we all right" for the first time, and
  the quote prints on one A4 page. Home on a phone is still a scroll, and

- **Office.** Quotes, kit from the notes and vans save a lot of typing,

**Learnings for the design system.** A role's view needs a state of its
own: "not sent to you" is neither locked, disabled nor empty, and a
button the role can't use shouldn't be drawn at all. Volume is a state
too: every list here was designed with ten rows and works with ten; at
three hundred it needs a summary first and the rows folded, the same rule
as "bundle the bad news". And a screen's top is part of "where am I": a
new screen that opens half way down has lost its title.
