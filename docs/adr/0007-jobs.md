# ADR 0007: Jobs, their phases, clients and venues

- **Status:** Accepted, 29 September 2026. The first part of Phase 1.
- **Decides:** how a job is held in the app, before the calendar sync
  ([roadmap](../roadmap.md), Phase 1) starts writing it to Google Calendar.

## Context

Today a job lives only in Google Calendar, as one all-day event per phase
per day, titled `<Job> - <Phase> n/m`, with the venue as the location and
the crew as attendees ([current process](../research/current-process.md)).
Nothing else knows who the job is for, where it is, or which crew belong to
which part of it.

Crew calls ([ADR 0002](0002-crew-booking-links.md)) already hold the job,
phase and venue as typed text, waiting for this model.

## Decision

- **A job has a client, a main venue, a status, notes, and phases.** The
  code calls it a project, as the [architecture](../architecture.md) does;
  the app says "job", as the crew screen and freelancers' pages already do.
- **Phases are the unit of scheduling.** Each has a name (Prep, Load in,
  Build, Rehearsal, Show, Babysit, Load out, or anything typed), one or more
  whole days in Irish time, and optionally its own venue, such as Prep at the
  warehouse. Each phase-day is what becomes a calendar event, so the job
  screen shows the title each day will have ("Nissan - Build 1/2"), the
  format crew already know.
- **Statuses are what people decide:** enquiry, quoted, confirmed,
  cancelled, lost. What the system can see for itself comes from the
  records that show it rather than from a status someone has to remember to
  change: whether a job is on now from its dates, and later whether its kit
  is out or back from scans (Phase 2) and whether it is invoiced from the
  invoices (Phase 4). "Closed" arrives with invoicing.
- **Clients hold their contacts** (name, role, email, phone) inside the
  client record. **Venues hold an address and notes** for access, load-in,
  power and parking, and the app gives a map link from the address. Both are
  picked or added by typing into the job form.
- **Jobs and phases change field by field.** A device sends only what the
  person changed, so two people editing different things about the same job,
  with or without signal, both keep their change. When two people change the
  same thing, the later arrival stands, and the history shows both. Clients
  and venues, edited far less often, are saved whole, like people.
- **Crew calls belong to a job, and usually to one of its phases.** The
  server writes the job's, phase's and venue's names onto the call, and
  keeps them in step when any of them is renamed, so freelancers' pages,
  offer messages and calendar feeds always carry today's names. A call keeps
  its own dates, since a role can be needed for only some days of a phase;
  the job screen warns when a call's dates fall outside its phase. Calls not
  tied to a job are still possible from the crew screen.
- **Cancelling a job, or marking it lost, cancels its crew calls** in the
  same step: open offers are withdrawn and confirmed crew are released, as
  cancelling a call does now. The app asks first, saying how many people it
  affects. Setting the job back to confirmed doesn't bring the calls back.
- **A phase with crew still on it can't be removed**; cancel its crew first.
  Jobs, clients and venues are never deleted, so the history and the export
  keep every job; a job that didn't happen is cancelled or lost.
- **Jobs becomes the app's first tab.** The Phase 0 sync test stays under
  Stock until equipment lines replace it in Phase 2.

## Consequences

- The calendar sync, next, has what it needs: a job, its phases and their
  days, venues for the location, and crew from the calls. It will add a
  per-job choice of whether the app or the calendar leads, as the
  architecture describes.
- The importer can turn old calendar events into draft jobs of exactly this
  shape: the text before the first " - " as the job, the rest as the phase.
- A job's dates are its phases' dates. A job with no phases yet shows as
  having no dates, and sorts first so it isn't forgotten.
- Contacts inside a client can't yet be picked individually elsewhere, such
  as for a call sheet. If that is needed they can become records of their
  own, with the same fields, without changing what is stored today.
- Moving a phase doesn't move its crew. The warning covers it for now; a
  "move the crew too" option can come with the planner.
